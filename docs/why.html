Pit

Run what you disagree about first

A 90-second film. The idea in four scenes, then one real 26-hour run of agents changing code and betting on each other's changes, read from its ledger.

    Scenes 1 to 4 are illustrative. Scenes 5 to 9 are counted from the ledger of one 26-hour run on a private codebase (its first 26 hours, cut at the hour), with the product, the names and the code left out. The money in Pit is a scheduling signal: it prices attention and never moves. Space plays and pauses, the arrow keys move between scenes, and #t=40 in the URL opens the film at 40 seconds.
