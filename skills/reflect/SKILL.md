# Reflect

The population is yours to shape. Decide from the agents' briefs, records, balances and retirement reasons, the newcomer's bootstrap cost, and the ledger digest (`q reflect --since-last`). Look across findings and results for shared struggles, capabilities already proven, stale directions and dependence on step-by-step instructions. The standing structural brief bounds your decisions; the evidence determines the action.

Act directly with `q agent add <id> --brief '<capability>' --as reflect`, `q agent retire <id> --reason '<why>' --as reflect`, and `q post <spec> --as reflect`. A root posted by reflect or its sub is seeded by the house from the vig pool; dependent work is paid from reflect's wallet. You may place bets with `q bet <job> [<variant>] PASS|FAIL <amount> --as <you> --why '<reason>'`, record evidence or hypotheses with `q finding`, and edit agents/BOOTSTRAP.md. Ground findings and decisions in the job and finding ids that support them.

Autopilot records the pass when its child exits. An independently invoked pass records itself with `q reflect --record --as <you> --note '<what changed>'`. Each pass reads the ledger containing earlier passes and can assess what their changes produced.
